            member.read-savings-balance  v1  ·  APPROVED

RECORDED BY hand-authored-fixture  on 2026-08-13
INTEGRITY   content hash verified

Look up a member by their 6-digit member number in CoreVue and return the current balance of their S1 savings share account.

TAKES
  memberId           string  matching ^\d{6}$  [pii]
                     The member's 6-digit CoreVue member number.

RETURNS
  savingsBalance     money  [pii]  — Current balance of the S1 savings share account, without the currency symbol.

OR ANSWERS WITH
  MEMBER_NOT_FOUND         No member record exists with that member number. This is a legitimate answer, not a failure.
  MEMBER_NUMBER_INVALID    The member number was rejected by the application's own validation.
  ACCESS_DENIED            The operator's role is not authorised to view this member record.

STEPS  (7)
  1  open http://localhost:3100
     verify: "Operator Sign On" appears
  2  type svc_automation into textbox "Operator ID"  (content)   [3 locators]
  3  type not-a-real-password into textbox "Password"  (content)   [3 locators]
  4  click button "Sign On"  (content)   [3 locators]
     verify: "Member Number" appears
  5  type {{memberId}} into textbox "Member Number"  (content)   [4 locators]
  6  click button "Search"  (content)   [3 locators]
     verify: "Member Detail" appears
     if "No member record found matching that number." → returns MEMBER_NOT_FOUND
     if "Member Number must be exactly 6 digits." → returns MEMBER_NUMBER_INVALID
     if "SEC-0412" → returns ACCESS_DENIED
     if "Your session has expired" → recovers (4 steps, ≤2 attempts)
     if "Scheduled maintenance window begins" → recovers (1 step, ≤2 attempts)
  7  read savingsBalance from the field labelled "Savings (S1) Current Balance"  (content)   [2 locators]

SUCCEEDS WHEN
  the Member Detail screen showing the S1 savings balance is displayed in the content frame
  verify: "Savings (S1) Current Balance" appears

No irreversible steps — this capability only reads.
